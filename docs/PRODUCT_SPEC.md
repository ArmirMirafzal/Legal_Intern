# LegalIntern — Product Specification

## 1. Product Overview

**Product Name:** LegalIntern
**Tagline:** AI-powered Legal Case Practice and Document Training Platform
**Target Audience:** Law students in Uzbekistan (e.g. Tashkent State University of Law, UWED, and law faculties nationwide).
**Primary Language:** Uzbek (Latin script) for all student-facing interfaces and roleplays; English for internal codebase, schema, and engineering documentation.

## 2. Core Educational Problems Solved

1. **Lack of Practical Fact-Finding Training:** Students study legal theory, but rarely get to interview emotional or reticent clients and witnesses to extract legally relevant facts.
2. **Failure to Spot Contradictions & Assess Evidence:** Students often take claims at face value rather than cross-referencing bank slips, chat logs, and witness statements.
3. **Weak Document Drafting Skills:** Law students struggle to draft structured, legally defensible court claims citing exact provisions from Lex.uz without hallucinations.

## 3. The YuridikSim Solution

- **Interactive Roleplay:** Fictional synthetic scenarios where students interact with AI personas (Applicant, Respondent, Witness).
- **Deterministic Scenario Engine:** Prevents AI hallucinations; information is only disclosed when the student investigates the correct topic and the persona has authorized knowledge.
- **Evidence Discovery Workspace:** Unlocks exhibits (e.g. EVD-001 through EVD-005) only when valid procedural conditions are met.
- **10-Section Legal Analysis:** Encourages rigorous fact-law synthesis.
- **Document Lab:** Interactive court application drafting with inline AI feedback on procedural and substantive flaws.
- **100-Point Evaluation Rubric:** Transparent scoring assessing questioning, fact-finding, evidence analysis, and legal reasoning.
