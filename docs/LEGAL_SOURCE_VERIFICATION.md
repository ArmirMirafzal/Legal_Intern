# Legal Source Verification — LegalIntern

## 1. Primary Sources for Uzbekistan Law

All legal references in LegalIntern must cite verified, currently effective legislation from official national databases:

1. **Primary Legislative Portal:** https://lex.uz/
2. **Supreme Court Practice Portal:** https://sud.uz/
3. **Tashkent State University of Law Educational Resources:** https://tsul.uz/ and https://moot.tsul.uz/

## 2. Verification Protocol for CASE-001 (Family Law)

- **O'zbekiston Respublikasining Oila kodeksi (Family Code):**
  - **96-modda:** Ota-onaning voyaga yetmagan bolalariga ta'minot berish majburiyati (lex.uz/docs/-104720).
  - **99-modda:** Sud tartibida undiriladigan aliment miqdori (1 bola uchun daromadning 1/4 qismi).
  - **103-modda:** Bolaning favqulodda sharoitlardagi qo'shimcha xarajatlarida ishtirok etish.
  - **136-modda:** Aliment undirish uchun murojaat qilish muddatlari.
- **Plenum Qarorlari:**
  - O'zbekiston Respublikasi Oliy sudi Plenumining 1998-yil 11-sentabrdagi 23-sonli qarori (lex.uz/docs/-1449622).

## 3. Unverified Reference Safeguard

Any legal source that has not completed official review must be marked:
`verification_status: "needs_legal_review"`
The application visibly informs students that content is for educational purposes and not formal legal advice.
